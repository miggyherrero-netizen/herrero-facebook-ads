# End-to-End Facebook Ads Campaign Execution & Review Process

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Type:** Campaign execution and review process  
**Participating members:** Marketing Campaign Team — Marketing Director (orchestration), Content Creator (static-photo production), and Brand Analyst (quality review). Facebook Ads Specialist/operator and business owner/approver are operational roles in the process. The Campaign Planner role is included in the designed workflow, but no substantive planner response was received due to an agent service error; the process does not attribute conclusions to that role. SEO was not assigned because this workflow is restricted to paid Meta advertising.

---

## TL;DR
- **Core goal:** Run a repeatable, evidence-led process from request and account study through approval, launch, monitoring, review, and documented next decision.
- **Key strategy:** Keep research/drafts separate from live execution; require explicit, version-specific owner approval before any account change or spend.
- **Measurement:** Separate Meta's exact reported result from qualified inquiries, booked/completed jobs, and financial outcomes.
- **Creative rule:** Static photos only, consistent with the owner's standing preference; no video concepts or tests unless the owner changes that direction.
- **Success standard:** A test can be called successful only against a confirmed service-area definition and an owner-approved acquisition-cost guardrail informed by job economics.

## Core conclusion card

| Item | Process standard |
|---|---|
| Target audience | People in owner-confirmed serviceable locations; age/interests only when evidence justifies limits |
| Primary channel | Facebook Ads through Meta Ads Manager; other Meta placements only when included in the campaign and previewed |
| Budget | No spend until the owner approves an exact total cap and period; PHP 3,500 over 14 days is an earlier proposal, not approval |
| Timing | Reverify account and facts before each project; after an approved launch, check delivery at ~48 hours, inquiry quality around day 7, and the full test around day 14, then refresh after the applicable attribution window |
| Primary business KPI | Cost per qualified inquiry and cost per completed job, using aggregate owner-confirmed counts |
| Meta diagnostic | Exact Meta result/event and cost per result, reported with objective, optimization event, dates, attribution, currency, and reporting level |
| Expected ROI | Not forecast until actual booked/completed-job economics and attributable revenue or margin are available |

## 1. Operating roles and decision rights

| Role | Owns | Cannot do without approval |
|---|---|---|
| Business owner / approver | Confirms service facts, service area, contact capacity, offer claims, total spend ceiling, availability, and launch/change decisions | Approval must identify the exact plan/version and specific live action; general intent to advertise is not approval |
| Facebook Ads Specialist / operator | Rechecks authorized account access; studies account evidence; recommends objective/KPIs/date range; drafts configuration; monitors and reports | Create, edit, publish, pause, change budget/targeting/creative/tracking, or spend unless specifically approved |
| Marketing Director / orchestrator | Keeps handoffs and stage gates clear; integrates planning, creative, and review | Does not override the owner's approval gate or invent facts |
| Campaign Planner | Recommends campaign goal, structure, audience/geography approach, budget proposal, timeline, and decision criteria | Does not authorize live spend |
| Content Creator | Produces truthful copy and photo-only creative variants from confirmed facts/assets; maintains version labels | Does not introduce unconfirmed price, offer, outcome, testimonial, credential, urgency, or service claim |
| Brand Analyst | Reviews voice, factual consistency, clarity, rights/privacy, and final version against the brief | A passed brand review is not launch approval |

The owner is asked only for real-world facts and approvals. The specialist recommends technical Meta settings and reporting ranges from the evidence rather than asking the owner to choose them.

## 2. End-to-end stage gates

### Stage 0 — Revalidate project context and access

**Owner:** Facebook Ads Specialist. **Output:** dated context/access note.

1. Read the current project brief, most recent study/reviews, campaign register, decision/change/performance records, lessons, and any handoff relevant to the request.
2. Reconfirm the currently exposed Meta Ads Manager session, correct Business Portfolio/ad account, readable scope, and whether access is read-only or permits actions. The saved 2026-10-02 check is historical, not proof of current access.
3. Do not bypass identity verification, request credentials/codes, or inspect unrelated Business Suite settings.
4. If direct access is unavailable, request only relevant Ads Manager screenshots/exports. Preserve received source exports unmodified when appropriate; keep derived files separate and minimize customer data.
5. Stop and resolve account identity/access uncertainty before interpreting account data.

### Stage 1 — Intake and owner-known facts

**Owner:** Owner supplies facts; specialist manages intake. **Output:** completed brief or explicit list of unresolved items.

Confirm, in plain language:
- Exact piano service/package and truthful inclusions/exclusions; whether any historical price or offer is still current.
- Cities/municipalities or travel radius served, travel fees, dates, and capacity.
- Customer contact/booking path, who replies, expected reply time, and ability to handle inquiry volume.
- Existing approved photos/logo/copy and any usage permissions or privacy constraints.
- Maximum **total** ad spend, period, whether fees/taxes are included, timing, and named approver.
- Business outcome that matters (qualified inquiry, booked job, completed job, or another explicit outcome).

Do not infer facts from campaign names, old copy, or historical settings. Unanswered launch-critical items keep the work in draft.

### Stage 2 — Read-only account and measurement study

**Owner:** Facebook Ads Specialist. **Output:** dated evidence-led account study.

Inspect the relevant campaign/ad-set/ad hierarchy, objective, status/delivery, budget and schedule, performance goal/optimization event, locations/audience, actual placements, creative/copy/CTA/destination, and relevant lead/message/tracking path where accessible. Select a useful reporting period from history and volume—often the latest 30 complete account-time-zone days, with a prior-period comparison only if meaningful. State the reason and exact range.

Record source, access/export date, account time zone, currency, reporting level, objective, optimization event, attribution setting/window, exact Meta result label, filters, and transformations. Do not add campaign/ad-set/ad totals together; do not compare unlike objectives or result definitions as equivalent. Distinguish manual transcription from native export. Empty means not reported, not zero.

For this workspace, the saved 2026-10-02 study reported historical conversation results but no verified bookings/revenue, and the visible PIANO dataset then showed no received events/integrations. The checked view showed a draft with PH targeting, PHP 880/day, and no end date despite a GenSan/PHP500 name. These dated findings require live revalidation and must not be treated as current settings.

### Stage 3 — Strategy and test design

**Owner:** Campaign Planner recommends; Ads Specialist translates to Meta setup; owner confirms business facts. **Output:** campaign proposal, not yet build authorization.

1. State one plain-language objective and the desired customer action.
2. Recommend the Meta objective and optimization event that match the confirmed contact path and available tracking. Example from the saved plan: Engagement → Messenger → Maximize number of conversations only if Messenger is confirmed and staffed; do not label a conversation as a booking.
3. Use one locally restricted ad set initially when budget/evidence is limited; avoid speculative city, income, or interest splits. Use only owner-confirmed serviceable locations.
4. Recommend placements only after checking creative eligibility and previews. Advantage+ placements may include other Meta surfaces; do not claim Facebook-only delivery unless the actual setup confirms it.
5. Present a proposed budget and test window with assumptions and downside clearly stated. Never copy a prior draft budget as approved. Do not promise ROI from historical cost per conversation.
6. Define the test variable. For the current preference, a two-photo test should keep service, offer, copy, CTA, audience, destination, schedule, and budget consistent so the photo is the planned difference.
7. Define the minimum evidence needed for a decision and the owner-approved cost guardrail before launch; small samples are inconclusive.

### Stage 4 — Static-photo asset and copy production

**Owner:** Content Creator drafts; owner confirms claims/assets; Ads Specialist checks placement fit. **Output:** labeled final candidates plus source assets.

- Use genuine, permission-cleared, full-quality still photographs of the confirmed service. The current preference is photo-only; do not propose video creative or video tests unless the owner changes it.
- Candidate pair, if both are needed: (A) technician at work on the confirmed service; (B) a close service detail or alternate angle showing that same work. If suitable originals already exist, assess/reuse them before asking for new assets.
- Ask for originals, not screenshots of ad mockups. Keep the technician/piano crop-safe, use clear light, avoid customer-identifying faces, addresses, paperwork, or unsupported before/after implication.
- Copy, headlines, offer, and CTA must reflect owner-confirmed facts. No invented claims, testimonials, credentials, prices, urgency, guaranteed outcomes, or stale historical inclusions.
- Prepare static crops for selected placements and inspect every final Meta preview. Saved Feed guidance checked on 2026-10-02 was 4:5, 1440×1800, under 30 MB; recheck current requirements and previews before use rather than treating that old specification as universal.
- Label assets/copy with campaign, variant, date, and revision; keep the approved version identifiable and do not silently swap assets after approval.

### Stage 5 — Brand, factual, and operational review

**Owner:** Brand Analyst reviews; Ads Specialist validates configuration; owner resolves factual issues. **Output:** pass, revise, or reject record.

Pass only when:
- Every service, area, price, inclusion, availability, result, and claim is confirmed and supportable.
- The message is plain, service-relevant, and consistent with the confirmed business offer; there is no unapproved endorsement or pressure tactic.
- Image rights and customer privacy are acceptable; crops are legible and do not distort the work shown.
- CTA and destination match the promised action, are staffed, and work end to end; inquiry questions support qualification without requesting unnecessary sensitive information.
- Campaign name agrees with actual objective, geography, budget, timing, placement, and destination fields; the final preview matches the signed-off creative.
- Correct account, Page, access scope, payment/billing setup as applicable, reporting currency/time zone, and no unintended duplicate/overlapping campaign setup; confirm the destination works on mobile and check applicable Meta policy requirements.

Use explicit dispositions: **PASS** only when all facts, assets, setup checks, and required approvals are complete; **HOLD** for missing facts, mismatches, unresolved conflicts, or fixable configuration/policy questions; **REJECT** unauthorized spend/access or a knowingly misleading claim that remains unresolved. Escalate budget, offer, capacity, and brand changes to the owner, and platform/legal policy concerns for appropriate review. If any material fact or visual is unverified, return it for correction. Brand approval is a quality gate only, not permission to publish.

### Stage 6 — Measurement and preflight

**Owner:** Ads Specialist. **Output:** completed launch checklist and decision-log recommendation.

Before requesting approval, verify in the actual setup:
- Correct account and Page; objective, destination, performance goal/event, bid approach, audience/geography, placement, and delivery dates.
- Exact total spend cap and schedule; reconcile budget fields with campaign/ad-set settings and names. Confirm the cap stays within what the owner means by “all-in.”
- Final ad text, image files/crops, CTA, destination, message flow, preview for each selected placement, and any tracking parameters/events actually available.
- Reporting definitions: Meta conversation/result, qualified inquiry, booking, completed job, and aggregate revenue/margin if supplied. Define qualification consistently (offered service + serviceable location + quote or scheduling intent); count one person once per inquiry in the aggregate business tally.
- Measurement limits and fallback: if web events/CRM/booking data are unavailable, use aggregate owner-reported outcomes and clearly state attribution uncertainty. Keep no unnecessary names/phone numbers in the workspace.

Stop preflight if a required field conflicts, tracking/result meaning is unclear, destination is unstaffed, a launch-critical fact is missing, or the proposed spend exceeds the approved ceiling.

### Stage 7 — Specific owner approval gate

**Owner:** Business owner/approver. **Output:** written approval or requested revisions.

Present one versioned approval packet showing:
1. Account, campaign objective and optimization event, customer action, exact audience/service geography, placements, and destination.
2. Final copy and static-photo assets/previews, with all claims and any offer/inclusions visible.
3. Start/end date and time zone, total spend cap, budget type, and whether taxes/fees are inside or outside that cap.
4. KPI definitions, review dates, decision guardrails, known tracking/data limits, and any pre-authorized contingency action (if none, no live response is authorized).
5. Exact requested live action: create/publish, edit, pause, or other change.

Approval applies only to the identified version and action. Any material change to spend, dates, geography, offer, creative, destination, optimization, or tracking returns to the relevant review and approval gates. Silence, a draft, a recommendation, or prior campaign approval is not consent.

### Stage 8 — Build, launch, and verify

**Owner:** Ads Specialist/operator, only after approval. **Output:** verified live state and logs.

1. Build/change only the approved configuration; do not publish unrelated drafts or use the existing GenSan draft as-is.
2. Recheck all final fields and previews in the actual account immediately before publishing.
3. After action, verify campaign/ad-set/ad status, delivery, budget, geography, dates, destination, and creative in Ads Manager; capture the verification time and evidence.
4. Update `ads/records/campaign_register.csv` with verified configuration and approved budget details. Append only actually executed changes to `ads/records/change_log.csv`, with approval, previous/new value, executor, and verification. Append the recommendation and approval/outcome to `ads/records/decision_log.csv`.
5. If publish/verification fails or state differs, stop further changes and report the discrepancy. Never say “launched” or “changed” until verified.

### Stage 9 — Monitored test and escalation

**Owner:** Ads Specialist monitors; owner handles service capacity and business replies. **Output:** dated check notes.

| Checkpoint | Inspect | Action boundary |
|---|---|---|
| First ~48 hours after launch | Delivery/status, spend pacing vs approved cap, location leakage, selected placements/previews, destination and message flow | Immediately escalate wrong account/identity, broken destination, out-of-area delivery, unapproved/incorrect asset or claim, rights/policy concern, spend/config error, or cap risk. Do not edit/pause unless the owner explicitly approves the exact action or the approval packet already authorized that exact contingency. |
| Around day 7 | Exact Meta results/spend plus aggregate qualified inquiries, service-area fit, response capacity, and unanswered-message issue | Identify data gaps and obvious operational mismatch; do not declare a creative winner from a few conversations or change several variables at once. |
| Around day 14 / approved end | Full test against owner-agreed guardrail, same metric definitions, cumulative spend, bookings/completions where available | Recommend stop, repeat, revise one variable, or consider scaling; owner approves every live action and any further spend. |
| After attribution matures | Refresh the report using the actual account attribution window and available business outcomes | Label late-reported events and uncertainty; do not claim causality when timing or concurrent changes prevent it. |

If no launch approval exists, these are proposed checkpoints only; no monitoring automation is authorized. Keep checks in the account time zone. Do not react to a single daily fluctuation.

### Stage 10 — Closeout, decision, and learning

**Owner:** Ads Specialist drafts; Campaign Planner interprets test implications; owner decides next action. **Output:** dated review and logged decision.

- Compare only like-for-like periods/entities with matching currency, time zone, attribution, objective, optimization event, result definition, and reporting level. State whether figures are exported or manually transcribed.
- Report source facts separately from calculations and interpretation. Include spend, impressions/reach/frequency and clicks where available; exact Meta result/cost; aggregate qualified inquiries, bookings, completed jobs, and revenue/margin only when supplied.
- Calculate cost per qualified inquiry = spend ÷ qualified inquiries, and cost per completed job = spend ÷ completed jobs when denominator is reported and nonzero. Keep missing values blank; never convert missing to zero. Flag small counts and tracking limitations.
- Reconcile platform-attributed messages with actual business outcomes; Meta conversation counts do not prove qualified leads, jobs, sales, or profit.
- State recommendation and evidence for each next step. Scale only when qualified-job evidence and owner-approved acquisition economics support it; otherwise stop or design a narrower, owner-approved follow-up test.
- Update `ads/records/performance_log.csv` at one reporting level per row and link the source; record the decision in `decision_log.csv`; write only evidence-backed reusable lessons to `lessons.md`. Do not fabricate placeholder rows or overwrite raw source exports.

## 3. KPI and decision framework

| Level | Measure | Use | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery diagnostic | Spend, delivery/status, impressions, reach, frequency, CPM, link/outbound clicks and CTR where relevant | Diagnose whether the approved ad is serving and attracting attention | These are not job outcomes |
| Meta result | Exact result label (for example, “Messaging conversations started”) and Meta cost per result | Compare within matching setup/definition and selected period | Not equivalent to a qualified inquiry, booking, or completed job |
| Lead quality | Aggregate qualified inquiries and cost per qualified inquiry | Assess service/area fit and meaningful customer intent | Requires a consistent owner/operator definition and tally |
| Business outcome | Booked jobs, completed jobs, cost per completed job, and revenue/margin if available | Decide whether acquisition is commercially viable | Attribution and incomplete reporting may limit conclusions |

The owner and specialist agree an affordable acquisition-cost guardrail from service economics before launch. Historical PHP conversation costs are context only; they do not establish a viable target. If outcome data is too sparse, report “insufficient evidence” rather than a winner.

## 4. Required records and versioning

| Record | When to update | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `ads/records/campaign_register.csv` | Verified campaign setup/approved budget | Store observed or approved facts, not proposals as if live |
| `ads/records/performance_log.csv` | A defined reporting period is reviewed | One entity level per row; exact result label and source/range/attribution; leave unavailable fields blank |
| `ads/records/decision_log.csv` | Recommendation, approval status, decision, or outcome | Distinguish proposed, approved, rejected, executed, and verified |
| `ads/records/change_log.csv` | Only after a live change is verified | Record exact before/after settings, approval, executor, method, and evidence |
| `ads/records/lessons.md` | A reusable lesson is supported by evidence | Include dates, source, metric definition, and limits on generalization |
| `ads/reports/` | Study, plan, launch packet, or performance review | Dated report; preserve the original export separately when appropriate |

## Risks & assumptions

- Service, geography, contact/booking path, reply capacity, offer/pricing, capacity, approved spend, and acceptable acquisition cost remain owner-known items unless separately confirmed.
- The October 2, 2026 saved account study is a historical snapshot; this process does not independently verify live account access or current campaign status.
- Historical data were manually transcribed for selected campaigns, not a complete native-export audit. Messaging results were not tied to verified bookings, completed work, revenue, or profit.
- The visible PIANO dataset's lack of events/integrations in the saved study does not prove that no other measurement system exists.
- Owner-reported photo success is a standing creative preference, not independently proven by the small/unequal historical ad samples.
- The existing draft's PHP 880/day and PH targeting are not authorized defaults. The previously proposed PHP 3,500/14-day test is not approved and is not a profitability forecast.
- Meta placement formats/features and platform reporting can change; recheck current requirements, actual configuration, and previews before launch.
- Privacy: retain aggregate outcomes; avoid unnecessary customer-level personal data. Never bypass access controls or store login secrets.
