For a consistent inquiry cohort, S = spend, U = unique ad-sourced inquiries, Q = Robert-approved opportunities, B = confirmed bookings, J = completed paid jobs, N = unique newly acquired customers:

- Meta conversation cost = S / Meta conversations, reported separately.
- Cost per inquiry = S/U; approved-opportunity rate = Q/U; cost per approved opportunity = S/Q.
- Cost per booking = S/B; cost per completed paid job = S/J; paid-media cost per new customer = S/N.
- Completion rate = completed jobs / bookings; payment completion rate = completed paid jobs / completed jobs.
- Zero denominator = “no outcome yet” with spend/counts; missing data = unknown, never zero.
- S/N is blended campaign cost per new customer when repeat work also occurs, not fully loaded CAC. Report repeat-job contribution separately; include acquisition labor for fully loaded CAC.
- Cash contribution = receipts minus refunds minus direct cash costs. Economic contribution also deducts Robert's chosen time cost once. Cohort contribution after ads subtracts S once. Contribution is not net profit; overhead/tax treatment must be explicit.
