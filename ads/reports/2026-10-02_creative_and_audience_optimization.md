## Recommended photo-only creative test

**Use static image ads only. Keep the same confirmed service, service area, Messenger CTA, offer, copy, and destination across both ads; change the image only.** Do not reuse the old PHP 3,500 offer, repair inclusions, endorsements, or urgency claims until the owner reconfirms them.

1. **Photo A — technician at work.** Reuse the original technician photo only if it depicts the owner’s real work, is full quality, and is cleared for reuse. Prefer a clean, authentic image of the technician carrying out the confirmed service, with the piano and hands/tools visible. Reduce poster-style text and avoid an unconfirmed price or claims.
2. **Photo B — closer service detail or alternate angle.** Use a second authentic still image of the same confirmed service, such as the relevant piano area, hands, and tool at work. Do not imply a before/after result unless there is a genuine, matched pair. If only one suitable original exists, use an approved crop/angle variation rather than adding video.
3. **Formats.** Prepare a portrait **4:5 Feed** version, keeping the person and piano crop-safe. The saved study checked Meta Feed guidance at **1440×1800** and under 30 MB. Make static-image crops for other eligible placements only after checking each final preview; no video assets are part of this plan.
4. **Copy and destination.** Conditional draft, only if tuning and Messenger are confirmed:

   **Primary text:** “Piano sounding off? Piano tuning in [CONFIRMED AREA]. Message R. Herrero Piano Sales & Services with your area and piano concern for a quote and availability.”

   **Headline:** “Piano Tuning in [AREA]” · **CTA:** Send message · **Destination:** Page Messenger, if staffed and approved.

   Replace the service and placeholders with confirmed facts before use. Ask for the customer’s city/municipality, piano type or a photo, concern, and preferred timing. Count a message as qualified only when it relates to an offered service, falls within the service area, and requests a quote or scheduling.

## Recommended audience setup

- Use **one ad set** limited to places the owner confirms are currently serviceable. Set a practical travel radius or named cities/municipalities; exclude areas the business cannot serve. Do not infer GenSan from a draft name or Davao from old ads.
- Start broad within that verified geography, all genders, without speculative income, piano-owner, or stacked-interest restrictions. A broad approach is consistent with the inspected Davao ad set, but is not proven to outperform alternatives.
- If a continuity setting is helpful, the inspected set used minimum age 25; this is a historical setting, **not evidence that younger adults perform worse**. Avoid adding further demographic limits without qualified-inquiry evidence.
- Keep Advantage+ placements eligible within the Meta campaign if photo ads preview correctly, and supply placement-appropriate **static-image crops** for Feed and other selected Meta placements. Check every preview and actual delivery; this does not guarantee Facebook-only delivery.
- Do not split a limited test into several interest groups or cities. Consider Page-engagement retargeting only after a useful warm audience exists and the owner approves the additional test; no customer-list upload is recommended now.

## Measurement and decision rules

Track Meta’s exact **Messaging conversations started** and spend separately from a simple aggregate count of qualified inquiries, appointments booked, jobs completed, and revenue/margin where available. Calculate cost per qualified inquiry and cost per completed job from those counts. Do not store customer names or phone numbers here. Review delivery/destination early, inquiry quality around day 7, and the test after the full approved period; do not make a winner call from a handful of messages. Set an affordable acquisition-cost guardrail from job economics before scaling.
