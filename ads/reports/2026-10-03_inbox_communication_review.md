## Findings and interpretation

1. **Booking reliability is the first priority.** C08 directly corroborates the owner's concern about autonomous bookings; C04 independently shows another owner apology and customer follow-ups. Keep AI appointment booking disabled. Even after a transfer, Robert needs to issue an explicit final confirmation; a generic acknowledgement can leave a customer believing the AI promise stands.
2. **Clients ask concrete questions and volunteer useful context.** Price, service location, symptoms and preferred days recur. C03 and C10 already supplied substantial details; asking for them again adds friction. Collect only missing fields, one at a time, after answering the question.
3. **Repair inquiries need a different branch from tuning inquiries.** C09 asked about specific parts and symptoms but received repeated tuning-price paragraphs. Acknowledge the repair concern; Robert assesses scope and price. Do not imply all repairs are covered by the advertised starting rate or that a distant onsite visit is available.
4. **Short does not mean vague.** Robert's natural replies often use brief Bisaya/Tagalog/English with ordinary courtesy. Preserve that warmth and brevity; do not copy repeated apologies, spelling errors, uncertain technical claims or ambiguous appointment acknowledgements. Length alone cannot be claimed to cause lost business from this sample.
5. **Respect message preference.** C06 explicitly preferred text; C04 mentioned an alternative messaging channel when calls were difficult. Call prompts appeared across multiple sampled threads, including a complaint. Offer a call only when useful and welcomed; do not insert a generic call pitch into every exchange.
6. **Existing customers should not restart lead intake.** C06/C07 needed progress or after-service clarification, not a new sales pitch. Route complaints, existing jobs and previously promised appointments to Robert with the existing context. Do not re-collect name/contact/address unless missing or needing confirmation.
7. **Other response sources still need inspection.** Historical ad context and an Oct 2 response showed PHP3,500, while the owner-approved current offer is starts-at PHP3,000 depending on location. These predate the configuration update and do not show that the update failed. An older message in C09 was explicitly labelled Automated response; this is evidence of a separate historical automation source, not proof of its current enabled state. Review ad greetings, FAQs, saved replies and Inbox Automations before activation. Do not change historical messages.
8. **Labels are not outcomes.** Some threads showed Qualified while the customer was waiting or requesting updates. One showed clear scheduling progress but no completed-job evidence. This review cannot calculate conversion, loss, revenue, response-time averages or advertising ROI.

## Recommended conversation flow — draft, not applied this turn

1. Identify intent: new tuning/cleaning inquiry; repair/moving; piano purchase; existing-job update; complaint/human request.
2. Answer the actual question in one or two short sentences, matching the customer's language. Never pretend to be Robert; be transparent if asked who is replying.
3. For a new service inquiry, use supplied city/barangay and symptom; ask only what is missing. Do not promise area coverage, travel fees or availability.
4. When the customer wants a quote, collect missing name, contact number and service address one at a time. A preferred date is a request only. Do not require email, serial number or photo for every tuning inquiry.
5. Say clearly that Robert must review the final price and schedule and that nothing is booked yet. Do not claim a notification was sent unless the actual mechanism is verified.
6. For existing jobs, complaints, repair-scope changes or previous promised bookings, stop the sales flow and make the case available for Robert's personal review. A complaint must not receive an unrelated price pitch or routine sales follow-up.
7. Robert's eventual confirmation should state the agreed service, exact date/time, location and agreed total/travel charge. AI must not produce this confirmation itself.

### Suggested wording

- **Price:** “Starts at ₱3,000 ang tuning, cleaning ug minor repairs, depende sa location. Asa nga city/barangay ang piano?” This advertises the supplied offer, not unlimited repair coverage; Robert confirms exact scope.
- **Specific repair:** “Noted ang key nga dili motingog. Si Robert ang mo-assess sa repair ug presyo; asa nga city/barangay ang piano?” Omit the location question when already supplied.
- **Distant area + date:** “Si Robert pa ang mo-check kung makaadto sa inyong area ug sa imong preferred date. Dili pa ni confirmed nga booking.” Then ask the next missing detail only if appropriate.
- **Details complete:** “Salamat. Si Robert pa ang mo-confirm sa quote ug schedule; wala pa ni na-book.”
- **Existing-job update:** “Noted ang imong follow-up. Si Robert mismo ang kinahanglan mohatag sa update sa imong piano.” Do not invent completion dates or claim he has been notified.
- **Customer says they waited:** “Pasensya sa kalibog ug sa imong paghulat. Kinahanglan ni ma-review ni Robert mismo; dili ko mohatag og bag-ong schedule nga wala niya gi-confirm.”
