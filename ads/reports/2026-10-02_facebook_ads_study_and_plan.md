## 3. Recommended approach for piano services

**Proposed goal:** generate serviceable inquiries that become booked and completed piano-service jobs.

**Objective:** start with **Engagement → Messenger → Maximize number of conversations**, if the owner confirms Messenger is staffed. That exact path is already available in the draft, suits an inquiry-and-quote service, and avoids pretending we can optimize for unverified booked-job events. “Engagement” here means the conversations goal, not likes. The past Sales campaign also optimized conversations, so its name does not prove it generated purchases.

Meta also supports Leads with messaging; it is a later option if a structured qualification flow and lead measurement are available. Do not claim Leads is unavailable or requires a website pixel. For this first controlled test, use the verified conversation path, then judge the quality of those conversations. [Meta lead-generation messaging guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf).

**Structure:** one campaign, one local ad set, and two **static-photo ads only**: a technician-at-work image and a second authentic service-detail or alternate-angle photo. Keep the offer, copy, audience, CTA, and destination the same so the photo is the only test variable. No video creative, separate interest stacks, reach campaign, or retargeting budget initially. The budget is too small to justify reproducing 21 creative variants across three ad sets before we have a qualified-inquiry baseline.

**Geography:** only owner-confirmed serviceable cities/municipalities or a practical travel radius. Do not assume GenSan from the draft or Davao from old ads. Broad adults in that boundary, all genders, without speculative income or piano-interest restrictions; propose ages 25–65+ initially based on the inspected historical setup, with no evidence that younger adults are inherently unsuitable. No customer-list upload.

**Placements:** propose Advantage+ placements within this Meta campaign only if the static-photo ad previews correctly in each selected placement. Supply placement-appropriate image crops for Feed and any eligible Reels/Stories placements. This can include Instagram and other eligible Meta placements; it is not a Facebook-only guarantee. Keep Messenger as the proposed single destination to simplify replies. Check every preview and actual eligible placement before approval; do not infer all delivered placements from Advantage+ alone.

**Budget proposal, not approval:** a **14-day test with PHP 3,500 total ad spend** (PHP 250/day equivalent), using a fixed start/end and lifetime budget if supported in final setup. This uses a historically observed budget scale; it is not a profitability forecast. If the owner's ceiling is lower, a PHP 2,100 total test (PHP 150/day equivalent) can be used, with correspondingly less evidence. Clarify whether the ceiling includes taxes/fees, and reduce the ad allocation to keep the total inside that ceiling. Do not treat a daily budget as a hard daily billing cap. Do not adopt the draft's PHP 880/day automatically.

**Success measures:**

1. Meta diagnostic: spend and cost per **Messaging conversations started**, keeping the exact reporting definition. Historic values are context, not a guaranteed target.
2. Primary operating measure: **cost per qualified inquiry** = spend / inquiries that concern an offered piano service, lie in the service area and request a quote or scheduling. One person contacting twice is one inquiry in the business tally.
3. Business result: booked jobs, completed jobs, cost per completed job, and revenue/margin when the owner can provide aggregate totals. A booking is a mutually confirmed service appointment, not a chat or price question.

Keep aggregate daily/weekly counts in this workspace; no customer names or phone numbers are needed. Agree the affordable acquisition cost from the job margin before scaling. Do not invent a CPA/ROAS target from the old PHP 3,500 price.

**Review proposal:** after any approved launch, check delivery/destination and spend after 48 hours; review inquiry quality on day 7; assess the full test after day 14, with a later attribution refresh where relevant. Avoid changing ads daily because of a few conversations. Flag out-of-area inquiries, unanswered messages, broken destinations or spend nearing the approved total immediately. All pause/edit decisions still require specific owner authorization; no monitoring automation has been scheduled.

### Proposed chat flow

Greeting: “Thanks for contacting R. Herrero Piano Sales & Services. To check the right service and availability, please send: (1) your city/municipality, (2) your piano type or a photo, and (3) the concern you want help with.” Then ask the preferred service date, provide a confirmed quote/inclusions, and confirm an appointment manually. Use three short starting prompts: **Check service availability**, **Ask for a quote**, **Describe a piano problem**. Do not promise same-day visits, diagnosis from a photo or guaranteed restoration.
