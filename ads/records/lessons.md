# Evidence-backed advertising lessons

## 2026-10-02 Verify settings rather than campaign names

The GenSan draft's name suggested GenSan and PHP500/day, while its actual ad-set Review showed PH location inclusion, PHP880/day and an ongoing schedule. Lesson: read actual fields before presenting a launch for approval. This does not establish why those settings were chosen or authorize a correction.

## 2026-10-02 Conversation results are not bookings

For January 1–September 30, 2026, the inspected April Sales campaign reported 332 messaging conversations started at PHP36.93 each, while its broad ad set optimized conversations. Events Manager's PIANO dataset showed no received events/integrations. Lesson: do not interpret the Sales objective label or conversation count as verified booked jobs, purchases or revenue. Other uninspected measurement systems may exist.

Source for both entries: [read-only study](../reports/2026-10-02_facebook_ads_study_and_plan.md), live Ads Manager and Events Manager inspected 2026-10-02.

## 2026-10-03 Messenger inquiry handling needs explicit owner confirmation

A qualitative sample of ten conversations active September 3–October 2 showed an AI-confirmed visit followed by a customer reporting they waited, another booking-confusion apology, repeated tuning-price replies to a specific repair inquiry, and existing clients asking for updates. Keep inquiry collection separate from owner-confirmed bookings; reuse supplied details and route existing-job concerns without restarting the sales flow. A transfer marker or Qualified label is not proof of owner response or completed work. The sample does not establish inbox-wide rates or reasons for every stalled inquiry.

Source: [inbox communication review](../reports/2026-10-03_inbox_communication_review.md), authenticated Page inbox inspected 2026-10-03. Review only; no client messages or live configuration changes.
